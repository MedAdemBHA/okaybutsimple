Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what Okay, but simple? (the "Service") collects when you connect your TikTok account, and how it is used.

1. Information we receive from TikTok

Only with your permission, through TikTok Login Kit:

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): display name, avatar and open ID.
  • Profile details (user.info.profile): bio, profile link and verified status.
  • Stats (user.info.stats): follower, following, likes and video counts.
  • Videos (video.list): your public videos and their details.
  • Posting (video.upload, video.publish): permission to send videos you choose to your TikTok account.

2. How we use it

Only to show your account and stats in the dashboard and to upload or publish videos when you click Post. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Videos you upload

Videos and captions are processed only to send them to TikTok and are not kept after posting is complete.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely by our authentication provider and used only to make requests you asked for. We keep data only while your account is connected.

5. Sharing

We share data only with TikTok (to perform your requests) and with service providers that help run the Service, such as our authentication provider, under confidentiality obligations.

6. Your choices

You can disconnect at any time in the Service or in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. After you disconnect, we delete your stored data. You can also email us to ask for deletion or a copy of your data.

7. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age for TikTok where you live.

8. Changes

We will post any changes on this page.
