Okay, but simple?

Okay, but simple? helps creators plan, upload and publish short learning videos to their own TikTok account, and see their profile and video stats in one simple dashboard.

How it works

  1. Click Continue with TikTok and approve the permissions.
  2. Pick a video, write a caption and choose who can see it.
  3. Click Post to publish it, or send it to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. Check your followers, likes and past videos on the dashboard.

We only post when you click Post. You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time.
